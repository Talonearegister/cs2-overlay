## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Hacks
- ESP — see enemies through walls with boxes, health, and names
- Radar — show all players on a custom radar overlay
- Triggerbot — automatically shoot when crosshair is on an enemy
- Bunnyhop — automatic perfect bunny hopping
- No Recoil — eliminate weapon recoil

### 🎨 Cosmetics & Unlocks
- Skin Changer — change weapon skins, knives, and gloves
- Custom Models — replace player models with any model
- Agent Changer — change agent skins

### 🏋️ Practice & Training
- Aimbot — customizable aim assistance with FOV and smoothing
- Wallhack — see enemies through walls with customizable colors
- Grenade Helper — show grenade trajectories and landing spots
- Backtrack — lag compensation for hitting moving targets
